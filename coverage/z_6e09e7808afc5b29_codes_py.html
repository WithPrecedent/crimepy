1"""The labels of the codes in the NIBRS master files. 

2 

3Each codebook maps the codes of a field (as they are written in the master 

4files) to their labels. The labels follow the reference tables that the FBI's 

5Crime Data Explorer publishes with its NIBRS downloads (such as 

6"NIBRS_OFFENSE_TYPE.csv"), and, for the fields that are only in the master 

7files (such as the batch header's population group), the FBI's "NIBRS 

8Flatfile Yearly Master Record Descriptions". 

9 

10Codes changed over the years, and each codebook has every code that any year 

11uses. A few codes meant something else in earlier years: 

12 

13* Race "A" was "Asian or Pacific Islander" before 2013, when "P" (Native 

14 Hawaiian or Other Pacific Islander) was added. 

15* Exceptional clearance "C" was "Extradition Denied" in the 1995 document 

16 and is "In Custody of Other Jurisdiction" now. 

17 

18Contents: 

19 CODEBOOKS: every codebook, by the name that `crimepy.nibrs.layouts.Field` 

20 uses. 

21 ACTIVITIES: the labels of criminal activities and gang information 

22 ("activities"). 

23 AGENCY_TYPES: the labels of agency types ("agency_types"). 

24 AGES: the labels of age codes of victims, offenders, and arrestees 

25 ("ages"). 

26 ARREST_TYPES: the labels of types of arrest ("arrest_types"). 

27 BIASES: the labels of bias motivations ("biases"). 

28 CIRCUMSTANCES: the labels of aggravated assault and homicide 

29 circumstances ("circumstances"). 

30 COMPLETIONS: whether an offense was attempted or completed 

31 ("completions"). 

32 DISPOSITIONS: the labels of dispositions of arrestees under 18 

33 ("dispositions"). 

34 DRUGS: the labels of types of suspected drugs ("drugs"). 

35 ENTRIES: whether force was used to enter ("entries"). 

36 ETHNICITIES: the labels of ethnicities ("ethnicities"). 

37 EXCEPTIONAL_CLEARANCES: the labels of exceptional clearances 

38 ("clearances"). 

39 INJURIES: the labels of types of injury ("injuries"). 

40 JUSTIFICATIONS: the labels of justifiable homicide circumstances 

41 ("justifications"). 

42 LOCATIONS: the labels of types of location ("locations"). 

43 LOSSES: the labels of types of property loss ("losses"). 

44 MEASURES: the labels of units of drug quantities ("measures"). 

45 MULTIPLE_ARRESTEES: the labels of multiple arrestee indicators 

46 ("multiple_arrestees"). 

47 OFFICER_ACTIVITIES: the labels of what officers who were killed or 

48 assaulted were doing ("officer_activities"). 

49 OFFICER_ASSIGNMENTS: the labels of assignments of officers who were 

50 killed or assaulted ("officer_assignments"). 

51 POPULATION_GROUPS: the labels of agencies' population groups 

52 ("population_groups"). 

53 PROPERTIES: the labels of property descriptions ("properties"). 

54 RACES: the labels of races ("races"). 

55 REGIONS: the labels of the Census regions of agencies ("regions"). 

56 RELATIONSHIPS: the labels of victims' relationships to offenders 

57 ("relationships"). 

58 RESIDENTS: whether a victim or arrestee is a resident ("residents"). 

59 SEXES: the labels of sexes ("sexes"). 

60 SUBREGIONS: the labels of the Census divisions of agencies 

61 ("divisions"). 

62 USING: what offenders were suspected of using ("using"). 

63 VICTIM_TYPES: the labels of types of victim ("victim_types"). 

64 WEAPONS: the labels of types of weapon or force ("weapons"). 

65 OFFENSES: the name, category, whom or what it is against, and group of 

66 each offense code. The names are the "offenses" codebook. 

67 OFFENSE_CATEGORIES: the category of each offense code (such as "Assault 

68 Offenses"). 

69 CRIMES_AGAINST: whom or what each offense is against ("Person", 

70 "Property", "Society", or "Not a Crime"). 

71 OFFENSE_GROUPS: whether each offense is in Group "A" or Group "B". 

72 OFFENSE_TYPES: the offense codes of each type: whom or what they are 

73 against (as in `CRIMES_AGAINST`), and "National Security Offenses", 

74 "Rape Offenses", "Sex Offenses", and "Immigration Offenses". 

75 STATE_ABBREVIATIONS: the postal abbreviation of each numeric state code. 

76 STATES: the name of each numeric state code (the "states" codebook). 

77 AGE_YEARS: the age in years of each special age code. 

78 

79"""
